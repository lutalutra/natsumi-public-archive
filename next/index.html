    Natsumi Sugiyama works across artistic research, editorial design, and information design. Her projects collect how people form a sense of place — through fieldwork, drawing, interviews, installation, and publication.

    She studied environmental design in Tokyo and Public Art and New Artistic Strategies in Germany, and is based in Berlin.
