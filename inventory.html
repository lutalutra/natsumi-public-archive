2017–2021Untitled無題field research, participant observationWeimar, Berlin and near Potsdam
Category分類
Other worksその他
Role役割
Researcher
Description説明
An activity carried on without a name, out of a need that could not be put aside. In GDR times, people who could not travel far built huts by hand and worked the land on small plots. Drawn to these places, where things quicker to buy are grown anyway and amateur DIY stands side by side, the artist went round looking at them, and for a time lived in a caravan, working the land herself.名前を与えないまま、やむにやまれず続けていた行為。東ドイツ時代、遠くへ行くことのできなかった人々が、手で小屋を建て、自然に手を入れてきた区画がある。買った方が早いものをわざわざ育て、素人のDIYが並ぶその環境に惹かれて見て回り、自らも野良仕事をしながら、キャラバンで暮らした時期がある。
