A Dot on a Line, Replaced and Continuing

2016 · Perception and memory of environments環境体験の知覚と記憶

A self-initiated illustrated archive of everyday spaces in a historic apartment building in Weimar, Germany, observing a daily life that continues as its residents change.ドイツ・ワイマールの歴史的な集合住宅で、住む人が入れ替わりながら続いていく日常の空間を観察し、イラストと記録のアーカイブにまとめた。
