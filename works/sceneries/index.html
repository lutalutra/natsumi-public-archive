SceneriesSceneries｜風景たち

2016 · Mobility and narratives of environments移動と環境体験の語り

A self-initiated project of field research, photography and editing on the relationship between landscapes and people, recording and editing landscapes in several cities from the point of view of how people relate to them.風景と人の関係をめぐる、フィールドリサーチ、写真、編集。複数の都市で、人がその風景にどう関わっているかという視点から風景を記録し、編集。
