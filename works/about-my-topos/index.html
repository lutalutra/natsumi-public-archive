About My Topos

2013 · Perception and memory of environments環境体験の知覚と記憶

How do people experience and remember a place, and build a relationship with the land? Taking as its site the typical suburban housing development on the edge of Tokyo where the artist grew up, the work made subjective ties to place visible in eight two- and three-dimensional pieces, based on local records and 100 sketches drawn from memory. The master’s graduation work at Tokyo University of the Arts, and the starting point of the later research on place and memory. Grand Prize, Mitsubishi Chemical Junior Designer Award 2013.人は場所をどう体験し、記憶し、土地との関係を築いていくのか。東京郊外の典型的な新興住宅地を対象に、地域の資料と、記憶を描き出した100枚のスケッチをもとに、場所との主観的なつながりを平面と立体の計8点の作品群として可視化した。東京藝術大学大学院の修了制作で、場所と記憶をめぐるその後の研究の出発点になった。MITSUBISHI CHEMICAL JUNIOR DESIGNER AWARD 2013 大賞。
