Unerased Reminders

2015 · Traces and interventions in structures構造のなかの痕跡と介入

What can be seen in the personal traces that remain unerased in a place of correction? Traces left in a former youth detention centre in Weimar, Germany, were observed and recomposed in drawings bound as booklets. The booklets were installed in the small, dark library next to the chapel on the building’s top floor, as part of the group exhibition There is an Exhibition in Prison. It is Called CRACK.矯正の場に、消されずに残る個人の痕跡から何が見えるのか。ドイツ・ワイマールの旧青年刑務所に残る痕跡を観察し、その気配をドローイングで組み替えて冊子にした。冊子は、建物の最上階で礼拝堂に隣接する、小さく暗い図書室に設置した。グループ展「There is an Exhibition in Prison. It is Called CRACK」で発表。
