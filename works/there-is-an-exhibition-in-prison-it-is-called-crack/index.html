There is an Exhibition in Prison. It is Called CRACK.

2015 · Editorial designエディトリアルデザイン

Design of the catalogue and the exhibition invitation (venue map and list of works) for There is an Exhibition in Prison. It is Called CRACK, an artistic research and exhibition project by the MFA programme Public Art and New Artistic Strategies, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, in a former youth detention centre in Weimar. The catalogue was published in 2016 in an edition of 200.Bauhaus-Universität Weimar の修士課程「Public Art and New Artistic Strategies」が、ドイツ・ワイマールの旧青年刑務所で行った芸術的リサーチと展覧会のプロジェクト「There is an Exhibition in Prison. It is Called CRACK」の、カタログと展覧会の案内（会場図・出品一覧）のデザイン。カタログは2016年刊行、200部。
