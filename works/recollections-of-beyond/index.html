Recollections of BeyondRecollections of Beyond｜回想：場所と人のあいだ

2022 · Mobility and narratives of environments移動と環境体験の語り

A collage installation recomposing, in sound and image, memories and experiences of places drawn from interviews. Fragments of accounts by people who have crossed continents and eras are combined into a new landscape where their experiences intersect. The work marked a turning point in the series Portraits of Places: rather than binding a place to a fixed territory, it sees place as where the trajectories of people who have lived through different times and spaces briefly cross.人々へのインタビューをもとに、場所にまつわる記憶と経験を音と映像で再構成したコラージュ・インスタレーション。大陸や時代を越えて移動してきた人々の語りの断片を組み合わせ、経験が交差するところに新しい風景を描き出す。
