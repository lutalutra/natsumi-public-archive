Phenomena, and Symbols of PersuasionPortraits of Dresden

2016 · Traces and interventions in structures構造のなかの痕跡と介入

Does fear of strangers have a connection to the love of one’s own place? Under this question, interviews with people met by chance in Dresden were turned into abstract portraits, using a new notation made from local materials (pigments from rock strata, grasses and flowers) and illustrations, and shown with wooden fixtures for looking into them. The work deals with how attachment to a city is turned into signs, and how readily those signs become tools of persuasion. Shown in the group exhibition Tomorrow We Will Explain (Kunsthaus Dresden and public space).よそ者への恐れは、自分の場所への愛着とつながっているのか。この問いのもと、ドレスデンの街なかで偶然出会った人々への聞き取りを、地元で集めた素材（岩層からつくった顔料、草花など）とイラストによる新しい記法で抽象的な肖像に置き換え、覗き込む什器とともに展示した。都市への愛着が記号に置き換えられ、説得の道具になっていく危うさを扱う。グループ展「Tomorrow We Will Explain」（Kunsthaus Dresden と市内の公共空間）で発表。
