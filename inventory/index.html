2025Portraits of (Places)Mixed-media objectMaxim Gorki Theater, Berlin
Description
How can an image of place, shifting through movement and change, be given form?

Four diagrams, assembled as bricolage, set three personal accounts of place against a fourth: a model of the instrumentalized symbol.

The narratives were recorded in interviews the artist conducted at the Maxim Gorki Theater, Berlin, in 2015, with people she met there. For a later exhibition at the same venue, the recordings were revisited and reimagined as if unearthed.

The theatre stands among museums where excavated objects are arranged and defined as national and human history. The work takes these accounts and gives them the form of finds, each carrying its own provenance. The fourth is the archetype of landscape mythology — mythologized as collective memory and enlisted in narratives that bind people to a particular territory. Set beside the three portraits, it serves as the comparative specimen.

Stone, wood, clay, found objects, pigment from soil, resin

2022Recollections of BeyondInstallation, video
Collage using interview audio, video and fragmentary materialsPola Museum Annex, Tokyo
Description
A collage installation recomposing, in sound and image, memories and experiences of places drawn from interviews. Fragments of accounts by people who have crossed continents and eras are combined into a new landscape where their experiences intersect. The work marked a turning point in the series Portraits of Places: rather than binding a place to a fixed territory, it sees place as where the trajectories of people who have lived through different times and spaces briefly cross.

2020In-Between Places and HumansField researchTokyo University of the Arts, Tokyo
Description
A long-term project gathering accounts of personal relationships to place from people of different backgrounds in several cities in Japan and Germany (Nagano, Roppongi, Dresden, Düsseldorf, Berlin and Weimar), and examining, through field research and making, the emotional ties between people and places. The doctoral examination exhibition showed not finished works but the traces of trial and the evidence behind them, such as original interview records. Written up as a doctoral thesis at the Graduate School of Fine Arts (Design), Tokyo University of the Arts.

2016Phenomena, and Symbols of PersuasionMixed-media object, exhibitionKunsthaus Dresden and public space in the city, Dresden
Description
Does fear of strangers have a connection to the love of one’s own place? Under this question, interviews with people met by chance in Dresden were turned into abstract portraits, using a new notation made from local materials (pigments from rock strata, grasses and flowers) and illustrations, and shown with wooden fixtures for looking into them. The work deals with how attachment to a city is turned into signs, and how readily those signs become tools of persuasion. Shown in the group exhibition Tomorrow We Will Explain (Kunsthaus Dresden and public space).

2015Landscapes NarrativesInstallation, researchMaxim Gorki Theater, Stiftung Mercator, Berlin
Description
How do different people’s memories of place intersect and form new narratives? Based on conversations with people met at the Maxim Gorki Theater and in the streets of Berlin, and with acquaintances living in the city, illustrations and diagrams were drawn and then shown to collect further stories. The installation deals with the relation between the accounts of people who have moved and landscapes that seem to be anywhere. Shown in 2015 in Bauhaus goes Gorki (Maxim Gorki Theater) and YOU SAY ART I SAY WHAT (Stiftung Mercator, Berlin).

2013About My ToposInstallation analysing and recomposing environmental memoryTokyo International Forum; The University Art Museum, Tokyo University of the Arts, Tokyo
Description
How do people experience and remember a place, and build a relationship with the land? Taking as its site the typical suburban housing development on the edge of Tokyo where the artist grew up, the work made subjective ties to place visible in eight two- and three-dimensional pieces, based on local records and 100 sketches drawn from memory. The master’s graduation work at Tokyo University of the Arts, and the starting point of the later research on place and memory. Grand Prize, Mitsubishi Chemical Junior Designer Award 2013.
